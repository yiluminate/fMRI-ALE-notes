# Methods, Results, and Figure Legend Outline

[Templates](README.md) · [Quality checks and reporting](../docs/06-quality-and-reporting.md)

## Methods

### Research Question and Protocol

Target population, task/cognitive process, baseline, direction, primary analysis, secondary analyses, registration or protocol location, and amendments: to be completed.

### Search and Screening

Databases, complete search strategies, date limits, supplementary searches, deduplication, reviewers, disagreement resolution, eligibility criteria, and screening-flow counts: to be completed.

### Coordinate Extraction and Independence

Sources of whole-brain results, original table locations, definition of Subjects, handling of reused/overlapping samples and multiple contrasts, and verification procedures: to be completed.

### Coordinate Spaces and Transformations

Original spaces, target space, normalization/template information, transformation methods, and previous transformations: to be completed.

### ALE Analysis

Analysis space, mask, primary correction method, cluster-forming and final corrected thresholds, permutation count, extent restrictions, and other settings: to be completed.

### Conjunction, Contrast, and Sensitivity Analyses

Definitions and input conditions for A/B/pooled datasets, independence, the contrast’s own inference method, the shared-region algorithm, directions, and purposes of sensitivity analyses: to be completed or not applicable.

### Image Display

Image viewer, template, statistic represented by the color scale, display range, left–right convention, atlas, and slices: to be completed.

## Results

### Screening Results

Numbers of retrieved records, duplicates, full texts assessed, exclusions and their reasons, included publications, and independent units: to be completed. Check that counts agree across stages.

### Statistical Results

Report clusters and peaks surviving the prespecified thresholds, together with their directions. Identify the corresponding tables and images, coordinate space, volume units, and type of statistic. Explicitly report when no clusters survive. Describe failed analyses separately.

### Sensitivity Results

For each analysis, explain what changed, which main conclusions remained stable or changed, and the limits of interpreting those changes. Significance in one analysis and nonsignificance in another do not by themselves establish a significant difference.

## Discussion and Limitations

- Conclusions supported by the available inputs and design: to be completed.
- Power, number of independent experiments, and task/population heterogeneity: to be completed.
- Original-study coverage, thresholds, missing coordinates, and selective reporting: to be completed.
- Limitations related to sample overlap, approximate transformations, software implementation, or contrast inference: to be completed.
- Distinction between planned and exploratory analyses: to be completed.
- Unresolved questions or information still required: to be completed.

## Materials Availability and Acknowledgments

Inputs, extraction tables, protocols, run records, outputs, and figures that can actually be shared, together with their access locations: to be completed. List the software and methodological publications actually used, and identify the sources of figures, tables, and other materials.

Also check search and screening reporting against the [official PRISMA 2020 checklist](https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020-checklist). This outline does not reproduce the official checklist in full.
